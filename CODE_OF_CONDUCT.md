# Code Of Conduct

Linewise welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of working. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and specific. Critique behavior, designs, and code rather than people.
- Make room for questions, corrections, accessibility needs, and differing experience.
- Describe evidence honestly and credit others' work.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, sexualize, or repeatedly target participants.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, correspondence, or project data without permission.
- Do not turn technical discussions into vendor-directed harassment or personal attacks.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, warn participants, restrict interaction, or exclude participants when needed to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate, explain the relevant behavior when appropriate, and protect reporters' privacy. Enforcement applies to maintainers as well as contributors.

For a confidential conduct concern, request a private contact channel from a maintainer without posting incident details. Until a confidential channel is established, do not post private evidence. GitHub's **Report abuse** tools are available for behavior violating GitHub policies, including concerns about a maintainer. Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith. The project does not promise round-the-clock moderation or a response SLA.
